I am a researcher at APM, specializing in seismology. My work spans classical seismological methods as well as emerging interdisciplinary fields including environmental seismology and glacial seismology. Email: junxie01@gmail.com

Name of this blog

SEISAMUSE is a blend of seismology, muse, and amuse. It suggests a small personal space where seismic research, technical notes, paper reading, and everyday thoughts meet. The name carries two meanings: “seis” points to seismology and the solid-earth world, while “muse” suggests reflection, curiosity, and writing. The hidden word “amuse” keeps it a little lighter, reminding me that research and learning do not always have to be solemn.

What you may find here

My notes, fragments, and records from research and daily work, including:

  • research notes and paper reading
  • seismology, geophysics, and scientific computing
  • small technical tricks I do not want to forget
  • experiences from using Linux, Python, GMT, LaTeX, and other tools
  • occasional thoughts on learning, work, and life
