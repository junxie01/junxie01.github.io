A letter for my child

2009-12-03 · 503 words · Topic: 随笔 · Tags: 儿子

Dear nuts,

Allow me to call you nuts the last time. Sorry, I have called you nuts for so long. Maybe you will say to yourself that, if I call you nuts, I would be the nuts father. Ok, stop thinking of that. You will have to get to know that, In the out world, I mean outside of your family, from us, nobody thinks that you are intelligent. They just take you as a nuts. And it is not necessary to tell all of them that you are smart. You will probably say that I am nagging again. Ok, I’ll stop.

But, there is actually a bad news for you. Do not worry, it is not about your stuff that hidding in my apartment. Listen, I have given you the tution for the first year, but, you know ‘but’ thing always comes the most important, which is I am not going to afford you any money any more. I am not going to tell lies that I am poor or some alse, that is suck right, as I taught you to be an honest man. You are already 18 years old right, ok including the year in your mother’s uterus. You know what I did when I was 18 right? I think your mother have told you. Ok, I am just kidding, but time has changed. You have to earn money for your college tution and life fare all buy yourself. Do not say any hard word to me, you are a tough guy like your daddy,Ok?

During the time in the university, you’d better consider what career you would prefer. Do you want to be a scientist as your father? Ok, I will not say that again. Life is like a box of chocolate, Ok, Ok, it is an old saying. I just want you to know whatever life you choose, it is your own choice which means none of my business. Haha, I am kidding, whatever career you choose I will be in yourside for good.

BTW, find a girlfriend as you like, which will make you more independent and responsible. Ok, do not yelling, I will not interfere your life.

This is the first time you go so far away from home, from us. I’m gald to tell you that, your mother and I are planing to have a baby, since your mother feels alone, and I also have nothing to play with. Do you want a little brother or sister? OK, do not be so shocked, I am just joking.

You can come back any time you want, of course you have to pay the traffic fare by yourself. And we will be there for you all the time. It is your home for ever.

Remember to call your mother from time to time, she must be missing you very much.

Hold your tears ok? I know you are grownup guy, an adult should be tough.

I am longing for good news from you.

your dad,
